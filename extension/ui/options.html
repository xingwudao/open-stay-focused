01

Website budgets

Sites in a group share one daily allowance.

Exhausted budgets cannot be relaxed during active hours. Allowance increases require extra confirmations.

A new boundary

One per line. Domains include subdomains. Paths are exact; use * to match any path characters. 0 minutes blocks immediately.

Always welcome

Allowed pages override daily budgets. A “block all websites” focus session ignores this list.

02

Active hours

Budgets apply only during these days and hours.

Today cannot be removed. Time-window and reset changes take effect tomorrow at this local time.

Active days

One range per line, like 09:00-17:00. 00:00-24:00 means all day. Overnight windows belong to their start day. Times use your device’s local timezone.

03

A deeper focus

A fixed commitment for the work ahead.

Nuclear session

Once started, this session cannot be canceled in the extension. Settings stay locked until it ends, even if you restart your browser.

Leave empty to start now. Scheduled sessions keep a snapshot of the current saved rules.

04

Less distraction

Keep the useful parts. Hide the rest.

These controls hide selected page elements. Site redesigns can affect them; they are not access restrictions or complete feed blockers.

05

Where your time goes

Foreground browsing and optional background audio. The last 30 local dates stay on this device.

Clearing statistics does not restore today’s budget or cancel a focus session.
